\section{Composed Duality Action and Observable Constraints}
\label{sec:composed_duality}

\paragraph{Two independent reflections.}
The strong-weak map acts on crossover strength \(\chi\), while orientation gauge acts on directed
labels.
Together they define a composed representative action
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{comp}}:=\mathcal{G}\circ\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{sw}}.
\end{equation}
On radial observables these operations commute trivially because orientation leaves \(\chi\)
unchanged.
On oriented observables they commute when orientation parity is preserved under strong-weak mapping.

\paragraph{Constraint for effective-temperature collapse.}
Suppose a saturated regime is parameterized as \(r=r(\beff)\).
Orientation pairing then requires
\begin{equation}
  r' = r(2\beta-\beff),
\end{equation}
so any empirical collapse in terms of \(\beff\) must satisfy the diagnostic ratio
\begin{equation}
  R(\beta,\vartheta):=\frac{r(2\beta-\beff)}{r(\beff)}.
\end{equation}
This provides a direct consistency check for representative fits in directional sectors.

\paragraph{Classical-to-quantum control exchange.}
Assume
\begin{equation}
  \chi(\beta,g,\theta)=g^2\chi_0(\beta,\theta),
\end{equation}
and endpoint behavior
\begin{equation}
  \chi_0(\beta,\theta)\to0\ (\beta\to0),
  \qquad
  \chi_0(\beta,\theta)\to\infty\ (\beta\to\infty).
\end{equation}
At fixed \(g\), this implies
\begin{align}
  \beta\to0 &: \chi\to0,\quad g^\vee\to\infty, \\
  \beta\to\infty &: \chi\to\infty,\quad g^\vee\to0.
\end{align}
So high-temperature weak influence and low-temperature strong influence are dual control sectors.
This exchange is computationally useful because asymptotics can be evaluated on whichever branch is
better conditioned.

\paragraph{Interpretive summary.}
The composed map is best viewed as a bookkeeping device that preserves physics while changing
computational viewpoint:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item strong-weak map exchanges the radial control branch,
  \item orientation gauge aligns directed representatives,
  \item composed action enables branch transfer with explicit parity tracking.
\end{enumerate}
